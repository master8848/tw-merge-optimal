### Regression note vs previous design

This run is the first after the matcher-only redesign (commit `239fdea`). The warm-cache
rows regressed vs the pre-redesign numbers (corpus was 1.32–1.36× in optimal's favor,
simple/heavy at parity): every class now resolves through the `BI`-indexed matcher and
the Map-LRU caches re-insert on every hit. The cache-off rows still win ~9.2–9.5×
(previously 12.5–12.7×), and the guarded bundle is smaller (40.91 vs 62.5 KB raw,
12.12 vs 17.1 KB gzip). Details: [docs/performance.md](../docs/performance.md).
